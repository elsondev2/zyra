# Zyra Browser privacy policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Zyra Browser is the Chrome companion to Zyra Desktop. It lets you chat beside a website and ask your chosen assistant to work with a selected tab or connected browser.

## Information used

The extension stores your browser display name, a randomly generated browser-profile identifier, appearance preferences, and your connection-pause preference locally in Chrome. Temporary connection credentials use Chrome session storage and are not included in the extension package.

When you connect to Zyra Desktop, the extension can provide website tab titles, URLs, and tab identifiers so you or your agent can find the intended browser and tab. When you ask the agent to work with a page, it can receive page observations, visible screenshots, and the results of browser actions. Prompts, attachments, and chat settings entered in the sidebar use the same Desktop services as a Desktop chat.

## Where information goes

The extension connects to Zyra Desktop through the loopback interface on your computer. It does not operate a separate hosted browser-data service. Zyra Desktop sends the information needed to fulfill your request to the AI provider and other services you select or authorize. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Review your Desktop configuration and provider policies before sharing sensitive content.

The extension does not sell personal information or use browsing information for advertising. Its browser access is used to provide the chat and browser-assistance features you request.

Zyra Browser follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. User data is used for the requested browser-assistance features and transferred only as needed to provide those features or as otherwise permitted by that policy.

## Your controls

You can choose an individual tab or share website tabs in the current browser connection, release browser access, pause the connection, or uninstall the extension. Closing the sidebar releases the individual tab access owned by that sidebar. Browser-wide sharing ends when you release it or disconnect. Browser restarts do not silently restore input permissions. Chrome internal pages, extension store pages, and incognito tabs are excluded from browser control.

Page observations and screenshots may contain sensitive information visible on a website. Browser input guards do not guarantee that every sensitive item on a page is hidden. Share only content you want processed by your selected assistant and services.

## Retention and deletion

Extension preferences remain in local Chrome storage until changed or removed with the extension. Its local activity view contains request metadata and clears when the extension worker restarts. Zyra Desktop may retain chats, attachments, and tool results under its conversation and data settings. Removing the Chrome extension does not delete Desktop conversation history or data already processed by a chosen provider. Use Desktop and provider controls to manage those records.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the extension's data handling changes. The effective date identifies the current version.

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact the maintainer through [the Zyra project](https://github.com/justelson/zyra/issues). Do not include passwords, connection tokens, or other private information in a public issue.
